# ![Triplet-D-Logo](icon-64.png) Hunter Island

**[Play in your browser](https://dimitrov-i.github.io/hunter-island/)** · [Game guide](https://dimitrov-i.github.io/hunter-island/docs/game-guide.html)

A treasure-hunting turn-based multiplayer board game about clever routes, unusual dice and plans that were perfectly sensible five seconds ago.

- **Play solo** against computer opponents or **invite friends** for multiplayer.
- Explore **four adventures**: The Oracle, Volcano Clock, Angry Men and X Marks the Core.
- Collect items, set traps and choose your moment. Optional character classes add a tactical twist.
- **2–6 players**, depending on the map. One explorer each.

---

## Island?

This is a fun game I made, inspired by hundreds of others. It's pretty basic. Most game mechanics and design I drew on paper (the physical thing, with pencils and markers) when I was around 12.

The short story: you are competing with your fellow adventurers on a quest. Supposedly, there is a big treasure at the end of it. Be the first to find it, and victory is yours. The island is large, and each map can take you along a different path. Each map offers a different game plan and requires a different strategy: pick The Oracle’s two-shrine race, Volcano Clock’s containment mission, Angry Men’s gold-funded circuit chase, or X Marks the Core’s expedition to a buried chamber. Follow the selected destination-arrival rule. There are hit points (HP), gold, ninjas, and more.

For more info: read the Game guide for all the rules on how to play. Or learn from experience – and just click the Play button.
